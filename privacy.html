Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

LinkHabit ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights over it.

1. What We Collect

  • Account information: Your email address when you sign up.
  • Social platform data: Read-only access to your saves and bookmarks from connected platforms (Instagram, Twitter/X, TikTok, etc.).
  • Usage data: Which links you open, your streak count, and reading history — used solely to power the streak feature.

2. What We Never Collect

  • Your social media passwords or login credentials
  • Your followers, following lists, or private messages
  • Payment information (we are free)
  • Location data

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate LinkHabit — specifically to import your saved links, generate AI summaries, maintain your streak, and send you the weekly digest email you opted into. We never use your data for advertising.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, ever. We use industry-standard service providers (hosting, email delivery) who are contractually bound to protect your data.

5. Security

All data is encrypted with TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest. OAuth tokens are stored in secure, httpOnly cookies — never in localStorage or exposed to JavaScript.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold on you.
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app settings. We purge all data within 24 hours.
  • Portability: Export your reading history as a CSV at any time.
  • Correction: Update or correct your account information.

7. Cookies

We use one essential session cookie (httpOnly, Secure) to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at privacy@linkhabit.app. We respond within 48 hours.
