Refund Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

✅ Our promise: If you're not satisfied within the first 7 days of any paid plan, contact us and we'll issue a full refund — no questions asked.

At LinkHabit, we want you to feel confident trying our paid plans. This Refund Policy explains when and how refunds are issued.

1. Free Plan

The Free plan has no cost, so no refunds are applicable. You can use the free plan indefinitely without any payment.

2. 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

All new paid subscriptions (Pro and Power, monthly or annual) come with a 7-day money-back guarantee. If you are unsatisfied for any reason within 7 days of your first payment, contact us at hello@linkhabit.app with the subject line "Refund Request" and we will process a full refund within 5 business days.

3. Eligibility for Refunds

A refund may be issued if:

  • You request a refund within 7 days of your initial subscription payment
  • You experienced a technical issue that we were unable to resolve within a reasonable time
  • You were charged in error (duplicate charge, incorrect amount, etc.)

4. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will generally not be issued if:

  • More than 7 days have passed since the charge
  • You simply forgot to cancel before a renewal date
  • You have already received a refund for a previous subscription period
  • Your account was terminated due to a violation of our Terms of Service

5. Renewals

Subscription renewals (monthly or annual) are not automatically refundable after the renewal date. We recommend cancelling your subscription at least 24 hours before your renewal date if you do not wish to continue. You can cancel anytime in your account settings.

6. Annual Plans

For annual subscriptions, refunds are available within the first 7 days. After 7 days, annual subscriptions are non-refundable, but you retain access to your plan for the remainder of the paid year.

7. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email us at hello@linkhabit.app with:

  • Subject line: Refund Request
  • Your account email address
  • The date of the charge
  • Brief reason (optional — helps us improve)

We will respond within 48 hours and process approved refunds within 5 business days. Refunds are returned to the original payment method.

8. Contact

Questions about refunds? Contact us at hello@linkhabit.app — we're happy to help.
