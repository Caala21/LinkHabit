Privacy & Security

We take your data
seriously.

Built with security-first principles. Read-only access, minimal permissions, no data selling — ever.

🔑
Read-only OAuth only

We request the minimum permissions required. We can see your saves — we can never post, follow, or act on your behalf.

🚫
Zero data selling

Your bookmarks are yours. We never sell, share, or monetize your saved content or reading habits. Ever. Full stop.

🔒
Encrypted in transit & at rest

All data is encrypted with TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest. Your tokens are stored in secure httpOnly cookies — never localStorage.

🗑️
Delete anytime

Disconnect a platform or delete your account entirely in one tap. We purge all your data within 24 hours. No questions asked.
